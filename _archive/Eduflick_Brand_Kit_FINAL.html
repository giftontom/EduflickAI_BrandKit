02 · the mark.

geometry · single shape · single notch
the eduflick mark

a feed.
cut by a play.

One shape. One notch. The square is the feed card — the unit of every short-form learning moment. The triangular bite on its right edge is the play — the moment a lesson begins. Bottom-weighted so it reads as forward motion anchored to the baseline. No spark. No flourish. The mark is its own punctuation.

11 · voice.

how the brand speaks · and refuses to

do · always.

  • Write declarative sentences. "Mitosis in 60 seconds." Not "Want to learn mitosis fast?"
  • Treat the lesson as the hero, not the platform.
  • Use lowercase for the brand, headings, and most UI. Title-case only for proper nouns.
  • Lead with numbers, durations, counts — 60s, 8 flicks, lesson 047.
  • Acknowledge the parent as: "a tomatrix technologies venture."
  • Set the italic only where a clause needs to breathe — once per surface.

don't · ever.

  • No gradients on the mark. No glow. No bevel. One colour, one notch.
  • No third hue. Indigo + paper + ink — or mono. Never both colour systems at once.
  • No emoji in the mark. No sparkles. No "AI" stamped onto everything.
  • No rotation. The notch always points right. Forward is a value.
  • No "revolutionary," "unlock," "supercharge," "next-gen." Earn the verb.
  • No exclamation marks. The full stop is the brand's punctuation.
