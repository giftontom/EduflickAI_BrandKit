01 · strategy.

story · pillars · audience · landscape · principles

"why does learning still feel like a chore when we carry a supercomputer in our pocket?"

Eduflick was born from a simple frustration: the world's best knowledge is trapped in hour-long lectures, dense PDFs, and sprawling video courses — none of it built for the way we actually consume content today. We reimagine learning as a feed: short, sequenced, personalised. Every flick is one minute. Every minute earns the next. The AI doesn't replace the teacher — it curates the path, so the learner stays in flow. A Tomatrix Technologies venture.

brand pillars.

five loads bearing ideas
brevity-first learning
Sixty seconds. One concept. Then move. Attention is the scarcest resource — we treat it with reverence.
AI as curator
The AI sequences, the human decides. Personalisation without the black box. Every recommendation is explainable.
feed as framework
The card is the atomic unit. The feed is the architecture. Scroll = learn. Every surface follows one interaction model.
outcome over content
We don't measure minutes watched. We measure concepts retained. The flick is the unit of comprehension, not consumption.
radical access
Free tier unlocks the full library. Premium adds depth, not gates. Education infrastructure should be open.

brand building principles.

five strategic rules for growth
i.
build in public.
Share the roadmap, the metrics, the mistakes. Transparency is the moat when your competitor is secrecy.
ii.
own the unit.
"Flick" must become a category. Like "tweet" or "story." Use it. Define it. Trademark it.

educator-first, always.

photography direction.

do / don't · moodboard words

do · always.

  • Warm, natural light. Golden hour over studio strobe.
  • Shallow depth of field. Subject in focus, background abstract.
  • People in context — learning, reading, thinking, discussing.
  • Negative space at top or right for type overlay.
  • Indigo accents in-frame: a book cover, a sweater, a wall.
  • 16:9 or 3:2 ratio. Cropped tight when needed.

don't · ever.

  • No cold, clinical lighting. No fluorescent. No blue cast.
  • No forced smiles. No stock-photo handshakes.
  • No busy backgrounds competing with overlaid type.
  • No heavy HDR. No oversaturation.
  • No screenshots of the app as hero photography.
  • No third colour cast — no green, no orange, no purple.

05 · voice & content.

how the brand speaks · and refuses to

clear. curious. never cute.

Eduflick speaks like a smart study partner — not a life coach, not a motivational poster, and definitely not a chatbot trying to be your friend. We earn trust by saying things that are true, specific, and useful.

do · always.

  • Write declarative sentences. "Mitosis in 60 seconds." Not questions.
  • Treat the lesson as the hero, not the platform, not the AI.
  • Use lowercase for brand name, headings, and most UI.
  • Lead with numbers, durations, counts — 60s, 8 flicks, lesson 047.
  • Set the italic only where a clause needs to breathe — once per surface.
  • Use full stops. The full stop is the brand's punctuation.
  • Be specific. "Learn the Krebs cycle" not "Master biology."
  • Address the reader as "you" — second person, direct.
  • Use active verbs. "Cut the textbook into flicks."
  • Anchor claims with specifics. "8 flicks" beats "a series of lessons."
  • Match platform register — same voice, different volume per channel.
  • End CTAs with value. "Start learning →" not "Click here →"

don't · ever.

  • No exclamation marks. The full stop is the brand's punctuation.
  • No "revolutionary," "unlock," "supercharge," "next-gen." Earn the verb.
  • No question headlines that can be answered "no."
  • No passive voice — "lessons are created by AI" → "AI curates."
  • No emojis in brand copy. Social captions: max 1 per post.
  • No weak modifiers — "very," "really," "quite," "just."
  • No future promises without a timeline. "Coming soon" is banned.
  • No comparisons to competitors. We don't name them.
  • No education jargon — "pedagogy," "scaffolding," "synchronous."
  • No all-caps for emphasis. Use weight, not case.
  • No gratuitous AI hype. The AI is a tool, not a miracle.
  • No hashtag stuffing. Max 3 hashtags per post, at the end only.

spark rules.

the discipline · how sparks stay precious
i.
earned, never bought.
No premium tier unlocks a spark. The only path is the work.
ii.
the mark is the trophy.
Every badge uses the eduflick mark. Tier shown by colour and ribbon. Never a new shape.

show, then fade.

Never "level up," "boost," "claim reward." Just: "you earned a spark."

co-brand.

eduflick × tomatrix technologies
eduflick AI
tomatrix

the parent · acknowledged, never centred.

Eduflick AI is a Tomatrix Technologies venture. In product surfaces, the Eduflick mark stands alone. In corporate communications, the parent is named once, in JetBrains Mono caps, at the bottom of the surface.

rules· Eduflick mark always leads.
· 1u vertical divider between marks.
· Tomatrix never appears in indigo.
· "a tomatrix technologies venture" — set in mono, lowercase, in footer.
partner logos · joint event
placementEduflick mark left, partner right, divider between.
sizeEqual cap-height. Never one larger than the other.
colourEduflick in ink/indigo. Partner in their brand colour.
neverLock the two marks into a single shape. Always separated by at least 2u.
investor deck
coverEduflick mark + wordmark. No parent mention on slide 1.
footerEvery slide: "a tomatrix technologies venture" in JetBrains Mono 8px.
last slideParent logo + Eduflick lockup, co-brand divider, equal weight.
neverParent logo larger than Eduflick mark.
academic partnership
placementUniversity crest left, divider, Eduflick mark right.
lockupL.04 mono preferred — when colour competes.
copy"In partnership with [University Name]."
neverUse the university's colour on the Eduflick mark.
white-label deployment
markEduflick mark replaced by institutional mark. Wordmark optional.
credit"Powered by Eduflick AI" in mono, footer only.
colourInstitutional palette takes priority. Eduflick indigo removed from UI.
neverRemove the "powered by" credit line. It must be visible.

implementation checklist.

for new joiners · brand onboarding
Read the manifesto. Understand the five principles.
Study the mark construction. Know where the notch sits and why.
Memorise the colour rule: indigo + neutral. No third hue.
Install the typefaces: Manrope, Instrument Serif, JetBrains Mono.
Bookmark the lockup usage matrix. Use only the approved six.
Review the voice guidelines. Rewrite one sentence of your own in brand voice.
Watch the motion storyboard. Know the 6 frames and 600ms duration.
Check the forbidden words list. Remove any from your drafts.
Understand co-brand rules. Never put Tomatrix above Eduflick.
Set up the file system. Clone the brand repo. Know where assets live.
Review the misuse cases. Know all 16 ways to break the mark.
Join the #brand channel. Submit your first asset for review.

brand review process.

how assets get approved
01 · submit
Designer submits asset to #brand-review with context: surface, lockup used, colour rationale.
02 · review
Brand lead reviews within 48h. Checks: misuse, lockup, colour, type, voice. Approves or flags.
03 · revision
If flagged: specific feedback referencing the brand kit chapter. Designer revises and resubmits.
04 · publish
Approved assets go to the brand repo. Changelog updated. Teams notified. Version bumped.

∞ · manifesto.

what we are · in declarative sentences

we make the lesson the unit.
not the platform. not the feed.
not the AI.

i.
brevity is respect.
A minute that lands beats an hour that doesn't. Sixty seconds, then we leave.
ii.
the lesson is the hero.
Not the algorithm, not the avatar, not the AI. The thing being learned comes first.
iii.
curated, then personal.
Experts choose what to teach. AI chooses the order. The learner does the work.
iv.
earn the verb.
No "unlock," no "supercharge," no "revolutionise." Say what is. Show what works.
v.
forward is a value.
The notch points right. The feed moves down. Always: a next thing worth knowing.
