04

do & don't

The guardrails that keep every brochure unmistakably Eduflick.

do
  • Keep headlines lowercase with one serif-italic payoff word.
  • Lead with concrete numbers — 12 weeks, 20 seats, 3 projects.
  • Let the page breathe: wide margins, one idea per spread.
  • Reserve coral for the single most important action.
  • Open every interior with a mono 00 · label tag.
don't
  • Add a second brand hue or gradients beyond indigo-ink.
  • Use stock photography — the system is graphic & typographic.
  • Set headlines in title case or stack three serif accents.
  • Round the mark's corners or add bevels and inner shadows.
  • Crowd the page with stats slop or decorative emoji.
