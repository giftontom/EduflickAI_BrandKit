05

caption recipe

The copy is half the post. Engineers, not marketers — and the brand strips emoji from finished captions.

# the shape — 4 moves Mitosis cuts a cell in half. Then again. In 60 seconds you'll understand why — the same way we teach LLMs, RAG and agents inside the cohort.   Free masterclass — register, link in bio.   #AIengineer #Technopark #Trivandrum #LearnAI #RAG #BuildInPublic
1
hook in line one
Short. Declarative. Often a fragment. It has to survive the "…more" truncation.
2
one clear cta
Always point to the masterclass / link in bio. "Register free →" — never vague.
3
tags below the fold
6–10 focused tags: geo (Technopark, Trivandrum) + topic. Not 30 generic ones.
4
no emoji, no hype
Never "level up / unlock / 🔥🚀". Achievements are sparks you earn. Brevity is respect.
