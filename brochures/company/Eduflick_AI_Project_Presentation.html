05 · proposed solution
eduflickAI

two modules, one platform

Eduflick AI integrates Agentic AI, LLMs & Machine Learning to automate content creation and personalize programming tutoring.

module 01
ai video generation
AI agents collect & analyze educational content.
generate structured teaching scripts automatically.
render to video with text-to-speech, animation & multimedia.
slash the time & effort of manual video creation.
module 02
adaptive programming tutor
online coding environment for C, C++, Java & Python.
LLM analyzes syntax, logical & runtime errors.
hint-based feedback instead of revealing the answer.
recommends practice by skill level & history.
supporting layer
faculty dashboard
tracks student performance & coding progress.
generates reports and learning analytics.
flags students who need additional support.
