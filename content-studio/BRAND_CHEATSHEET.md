# Eduflick AI — Brand Cheat Sheet (self-contained)

> **This is the only context a model needs to write on-brand Eduflick AI copy.**
> It is deliberately short and complete. Paste it whole into any model. Don't summarize it
> for the model — small models lose the rules when you compress them.

## 0. What Eduflick AI is

Eduflick AI (a venture of **Tomatrix Technologies Pvt Ltd**) reimagines learning as a **feed**.
It cuts any source — a lecture, a paper, a video — into sequenced, personalised **one-minute
"flicks."** The pitch: *the doom-scroll antidote.* AI curates the path (it sequences; the human
decides) so the learner stays in flow.

**There are two faces to the brand. Know which one you're writing for:**

| Face | What it is | When you're writing for it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The product (vision)** | The consumer **learning-feed app** — flick cards, the 60-second player, the **Sparks** gamification system. | Product marketing, app store, the "a feed for thinking" story. |
| **The program (active business)** | The **Full-Stack AI Engineer Program** — an in-person, capped-at-20 cohort run at Technopark, Trivandrum. This is what's being sold right now. | **Most marketing tasks.** The 6-week sprint to fill the Pioneer Cohort. |

Both share one visual + verbal system. **If a task doesn't say which, assume the program.**

## 1. Voice — how Eduflick talks

**Confident, technical, no fluff. Talk like engineers, not marketers.** Brevity is a value —
*"brevity is respect."*

- **Short. Declarative. Often fragments. Cut to the verb.**
  *"Mitosis cuts a cell in half. Then it does it again. In 60 seconds, you'll understand why."*
- **Speak to *you*.** ("pull down to retry," "your next 3 flicks are ready"). The brand is *we*.
  Never "the user," never "valued customer," never "dear students."
- **Numbers as proof, not adjectives.** `60s`, `8 flicks`, `78% retention`, `20 seats`,
  `₹49,000`, `12 weeks`, `3 projects`. Concrete beats hype. Replace "amazing results" with a number.
- **Own the unit.** "Flick" is the trademark noun: 1 flick = 1 concept = 60 seconds. Use it as a
  verb/category too ("flick through," "your feed").
- **The serif accent.** Exactly **one or two words** of a headline are set in *serif italic* and
  tinted indigo — the emotional beat inside a tight sans line. In copy you signal it with
  `*asterisks*` (e.g. `a feed for *thinking*`). Never italicize the whole line; never the verb-heavy parts.

## 2. Voice — hard rules (these are the ones models break)

| ✗ Never | ✓ Instead |
| --- | --- |
| Emoji in finished brand copy 🧠✨🚀 | No emoji. Use a mono label or a number. |
| "level up", "unlock", "boost", "claim your reward", "gamechanger", "supercharge" | Achievements are **Sparks you earn**: "you earned a spark." |
| Exclamation-mark hype ("Don't miss out!!!") | Calm scarcity with a number: "7 of 20 seats left." |
| ALL-CAPS sentences for emphasis | Use a mono UPPERCASE *label*, not shouting in body copy. |
| Vague claims ("best course", "world-class", "revolutionary") | A concrete proof: "3 deployed projects", "capped at 20". |
| "Hurry!", "Limited time!", marketer urgency | State the fact: "seats close [[DATE]]." |
| Title Case Headlines | lowercase display headlines (see casing below). |
| Hashtag soup (#learn #study #motivation #grind) | 3–6 precise tags. See § 7. |
| Inventing a date/price/link/name | Leave a `[[PLACEHOLDER]]` — only the FACTS block supplies these. |

## 3. Casing (this is a brand signature — get it right)

- **Display headlines & headings: all lowercase.** `learn anything in sixty seconds.`
- **Mono labels / eyebrows / meta: UPPERCASE, wide tracking.** `LESSON 047 · 60 SECONDS`,
  `PIONEER COHORT 01`, `FREE MASTERCLASS`.
- **Body copy: normal sentence case.**
- **The wordmark: "eduflick AI"** — `eduflick` lowercase + a space + **uppercase `AI`** (the
  indigo accent; white on dark/indigo, indigo on paper). Prefer the spaced form in copy.

## 10. Visual quick-ref (for when you also spec the design)

- **One hue:** indigo `#5B5BF0` (primary), accent `#8B97FF`, indigo-ink `#0B0822`. **Never a third hue.**
- **Neutrals:** warm paper `#F5F2EA`, cool ink `#0A0B10`. **Dark is default**; paper for documents.
- **Coral `#FF6E5A`:** semantic flag only — CTAs, scarcity ("seats left"), errors. Used sparingly,
  never decoratively.
- **Fonts:** **Manrope** (display/UI, 800–900, lowercase, tight tracking), **Instrument Serif**
  (italic accent words only), **JetBrains Mono** (UPPERCASE labels, meta, numbers, code).
- **No stock photos.** Show code, deployed UIs, the mark, data, or editorial type. Faint dot/line
  grids over indigo; the brand glow `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(91,91,240,.35)`.
- **The mark:** a square "feed card" with a triangular notch on its right edge (the "play"). One
  shape, one notch. Never distort, rotate, recolor, or add glows/bevels to it.
- **Co-brand:** Eduflick AI is **a Tomatrix Technologies venture** — the legal name is always
  proper-cased "Tomatrix Technologies Pvt Ltd." The Eduflick mark *leads*; Tomatrix supports and is
  never recolored to indigo. Footer attribution: "a Tomatrix Technologies venture" (mono).
- Render with `../design-system/colors_and_type.css` + the collateral kits; load the 3 Google fonts.
