## 2. Pitch deck & presentation architecture

### The disparity (the problem)

- **Systemic failure:** India's outdated, exam-centric education system leaves students disengaged
  and burnt out by treating learning as a chore of rote memorization.
- **Creator bottleneck:** Educators and teachers remain structurally constrained by a lack of
  modern, intuitive, and scalable digital resources to build interactive content.

### The solution

Eduflick AI bridges the gap between creation and consumption. We empower teachers with automated,
in-house **AI-driven course-creation tools** while delivering an engaging, gamified, and
**personalized mobile learning experience** for every student.

### The target ecosystem

- **Students:** quick subject revisions, stress-free test prep, and hyper-engaging, bite-sized
  content built for long-term knowledge retention.
- **Lifelong learners:** the ability to upskill anytime, anywhere via personalized learning paths
  tailored to diverse modern tech and creative interests.

### Product features & mechanics

- **AI-generated microlearning videos:** dense lectures and textbooks sliced into bite-sized,
  multilingual micro-flicks designed for rapid, friction-free retention.
- **AI real-time mentor:** on-demand, conversational Q&A support offering personalized
  recommendations and instant course correction.
- **Gamification engine:** high-retention hooks — leaderboards, micro-rewards, streaks, and
  community challenges — to keep users in flow.
- **Progress tracking:** real-time analytics dashboards letting both learners and creators monitor
  conceptual mastery.

## 3. Brand & course launch grid (social media)

A single, deliberately-composed Instagram profile grid: **two continuous indigo-gradient murals.**
The bottom 3×3 (9 tiles) is the **brand mural** (awareness / release); the top row (3 tiles) is the
**course mural** (the Full-Stack AI offline course launch).

### 3.0 How to assemble & post the grid

```
ROW 1  TOP    │ TL │ TC │ TR │  ← COURSE mural — one indigo image split 3 ways (posted last)
ROW 2  UPPER  │ UL │ UC │ UR │
ROW 3  MID    │ ML │ MC │ MR │  ← BRAND mural — one indigo image split 9 ways (posted first)
ROW 4  BOTTOM │ BL │ BC │ BR │
```

- **Sizing:** every tile 1080×1350 (4:5). Build each mural's master art first (brand ≈3240×4050,
  course ≈3240×1350), then slice into 1080×1350 tiles. The gradient + faint dot grid bleed across
  seams; **no word/logo straddles a seam.**
- **Safe zone:** keep all critical content (headline, mark, CTA, intake number, eyebrow) inside the
  **centered 1080×1080 square** — ≥135 px from top & bottom, ≥96 px sides. Nothing critical at the
  very top or bottom of the frame (survives both the new 3:4 grid crop and the legacy 1:1 crop).
- **Posting waves** (Instagram stacks newest top-left, so post in reverse — one full row per
  session, **right → center → left**, rows **bottom → top** — so a row never sits half-built):

  | Wave | Session | Tiles (R→C→L) | Grid after |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | Day 1 | BR → BC → BL | brand mural shows 3 |
  | 2 | Day 2 | MR → MC → ML | brand mural shows 6 |
  | 3 | Day 3 | UR → UC → UL | brand mural complete (9) |
  | 4 | Launch day | TR → TC → TL | grid complete (12) |

### Style parameters applied

- Lowercase aesthetic across brand prose · exactly one *italic serif accent word* per hook · hooks
  ≤125 characters · 3–6 hashtags per post · zero emoji · zero forbidden filler words.
- **Render with:** `../design-system/collateral/instagram-kit.html` (canvases + launch
  grid), `../design-system/recipes/instagram-post.md` + `snippets.md`.
- **Assembled mural preview:** `../design-system/collateral/launch-grid.html` — shows all
  12 tiles as the two continuous indigo murals, with a 1080² safe-zone toggle.
